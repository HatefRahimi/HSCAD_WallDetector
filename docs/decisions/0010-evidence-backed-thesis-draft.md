## Context

The author requested an introduction and all thesis text that can already be
written. This explicitly overrides the earlier author-only drafting restriction
for this writing task, including the discussion and conclusion where existing
evidence supports them. The draft remains subject to independent review.

The author also reported an online compilation timeout. A successful local build
does not establish that the online service's time limit is met.

## Decision

Draft the introduction and the supported portions of all existing chapters.
Preserve the supplied FAU/PRL thesis class and existing chapter files. Replace
expensive graphical draft notes with plain text notes and retain a complete source
archive for upload. Add verified primary literature to the bibliography before
citing it. Generate numerical tables from recorded public-data metrics.

The research framing follows decision 0008: training uses FloorPlanCAD only and
industrial drawings are reserved for external evaluation after conversion. The
original research question in AGENTS.md has not been edited by this writing task.

## Author-provided motivation and scope

The project brief identifies industrial DWG floor-plan interpretation as the
application, with walls as the primary target and doors and windows as secondary
targets. Walls are prioritised because they are common and expensive to redraw
manually. The question about geometry and context without layer names remains a
research objective. Conversion of detected geometry into valid HSCAD objects is
the planned practical check. These statements record the author's supplied brief;
they are not measurements of the archive.

## Evidence

The author's explicit request in this session authorizes the expanded draft.
Decisions 0005 to 0009 and their recorded public audit runs support the current
data and evaluation chapters. There is no recorded model-training result or
industrial evaluation result at the time of this decision.

## Consequences

- Preliminary data findings may be discussed, but model performance and industrial
  transfer must not be claimed before evaluation.
- A draft abstract may state the objective, protocol and completed data audits.
  Final model findings remain an explicit gap.
- Numerical tables are generated by a reporting script; no result is typed into
  chapter prose.
- The introduction restates the public-training/external-evaluation question. This
  framing needs author review alongside the unchanged question in AGENTS.md.
- Compile-time measurements describe the local build only. Online compilation
  remains subject to the provider's environment and time limit.
