\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{ch:conclusion}

% source: docs/decisions/0008-floorplancad-only-training.md
The thesis investigates semantic classification learned from public vector
floor-plan drawings, with industrial drawings reserved for external evaluation
after conversion into a compatible representation.
% source: docs/decisions/0009-element-metrics.md
Its comparison is defined at the level of geometric entities, using wall
precision, recall and the harmonic-mean \(F\)-score as the primary measures and
reporting doors and windows separately.
% source: docs/decisions/0010-evidence-backed-thesis-draft.md
The intended practical check is the conversion of detected geometry into valid
\gls{hscad} wall objects.

% source: runs/2026-10-26-floorplancad-parse/metrics.json
The completed parsing run records the available public contents and entity
inventory without reported file failures.
% source: docs/decisions/0005-floorplancad-class-mapping.md
% source: docs/decisions/0006-floorplancad-split-by-source-group.md
The accompanying audits establish a release-specific class interpretation and
identify the source overlap and exact duplicates that motivate a separate
evaluation partition.
% source: docs/decisions/0007-floorplancad-fold-assignment.md
That partition is fixed by a recorded source-group assignment.

% source: docs/decisions/0010-evidence-backed-thesis-draft.md
These findings establish the current data preparation and evaluation design,
while the questions of learnability from geometry, comparative model performance
and transfer to industrial drawings remain unanswered by the available runs.
% source: docs/decisions/0008-floorplancad-only-training.md
External evaluation still requires the conversion procedure and reference-label
recovery to be defined.
% source: docs/decisions/0010-evidence-backed-thesis-draft.md
The final conclusion must be completed from those measurements.
