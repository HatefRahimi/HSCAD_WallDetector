\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}

\section{Motivation}
\label{sec:motivation}
% source: docs/decisions/0010-evidence-backed-thesis-draft.md
Industrial floor-plan interpretation is the application considered in this thesis,
with the recognition of walls as the primary step towards converting drawing
geometry into objects that can be used in facility planning.
% source: docs/decisions/0010-evidence-backed-thesis-draft.md
Walls are prioritised in the project brief because they are common and expensive
to redraw manually; doors and windows are secondary targets, and valid
\gls{hscad} wall generation is the intended practical check.
% source: docs/decisions/0010-evidence-backed-thesis-draft.md
The geometry has to be given a meaning.

% source: docs/decisions/0008-floorplancad-only-training.md
The experimental design separates the source used for learning from the drawings
on which industrial applicability is to be assessed: FloorPlanCAD supplies the
training data, while industrial drawings are reserved for external evaluation
after conversion.
% source: docs/decisions/0008-floorplancad-only-training.md
This makes the transfer from public drawings to industrial drawings the central
empirical question of the current design.
% source: docs/decisions/0008-floorplancad-only-training.md
Training on the industrial archive is outside this scope.

\section{Problem Definition}
\label{sec:problem-definition}
% source: docs/decisions/0009-element-metrics.md
The prediction target is the semantic class of each geometric entity in a drawing,
with wall, door, window and \texttt{other} forming the output classes used in the
evaluation protocol.
% source: docs/decisions/0009-element-metrics.md
Lines, arcs, circles and ellipses are scored, while text is excluded because the
downloaded public release provides no semantic labels for its text entities.
% source: docs/decisions/0005-floorplancad-class-mapping.md
The association between the release's semantic identifiers and these output
classes is established through a separate class audit, rather than assumed from
tables accompanying a different implementation.

% source: docs/decisions/0009-element-metrics.md
The unit of prediction also fixes the unit of comparison: a vector method returns
entity labels directly, whereas a raster method must first map its pixel
predictions back to the vector entities being scored.
% source: docs/decisions/0009-element-metrics.md
Wall precision, recall and the harmonic-mean \(F\)-score are the primary measures.
% source: docs/decisions/0009-element-metrics.md
Doors and windows are reported separately, so that their performance does not
change the meaning of the wall score.

\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:research-questions}
% source: docs/decisions/0008-floorplancad-only-training.md
The central question is whether a model trained on public vector floor-plan
drawings can recognise semantic classes in industrial drawings after a compatible
conversion, without training on the industrial archive.
% source: docs/decisions/0010-evidence-backed-thesis-draft.md
The geometry-only objective asks whether geometry and its surrounding context
carry enough information for this classification when layer names are unavailable
or unreliable.
% source: docs/decisions/0008-floorplancad-only-training.md
Both questions require an external evaluation whose inputs and reference labels
have been defined independently of the model predictions.

% source: docs/decisions/0005-floorplancad-class-mapping.md
The reliability of the public labels is part of the problem, because an incorrect
class mapping would change what the model is trained and evaluated to recognise.
% source: docs/decisions/0006-floorplancad-split-by-source-group.md
The independence of held-out drawings is equally consequential: the recorded
source audit identifies related drawings and exact duplicates spanning the
official release folders.
% source: docs/decisions/0007-floorplancad-fold-assignment.md
The experiments therefore use a frozen assignment of complete source groups.

\section{Objectives and Scope}
\label{sec:objectives}
% source: docs/decisions/0010-evidence-backed-thesis-draft.md
The intended outcome is a comparison of methods for assigning semantic classes to
drawing geometry, followed by an assessment of transfer to industrial drawings
and a practical check of wall generation.
% source: docs/decisions/0008-floorplancad-only-training.md
The external evaluation constrains which inputs may be used, since the conversion
from industrial drawings has to reproduce the representation used for learning.
% source: docs/decisions/0010-evidence-backed-thesis-draft.md
These are research objectives, rather than established performance claims.

% source: runs/2026-10-26-floorplancad-parse/metrics.json
The completed public-data work includes a parsing run reporting processed content,
entity types and failures.
% source: docs/decisions/0005-floorplancad-class-mapping.md
% source: docs/decisions/0006-floorplancad-split-by-source-group.md
% source: docs/decisions/0007-floorplancad-fold-assignment.md
The class interpretation, source grouping and fold assignment are also recorded,
providing the data preparation on which subsequent experiments can be based.
% source: docs/decisions/0010-evidence-backed-thesis-draft.md
Model training, the representation comparison and industrial transfer remain to
be measured; the present draft reports the completed audits and leaves those
empirical answers open.
